Ricard Marxer

Professor, CNRS

  • Université de Toulon ·

Biography

Ricard Marxer studies what machines can learn by listening. His research spans speech and music as well as bioacoustics, where recordings of animal calls can reveal patterns of vocal activity. He develops and evaluates methods that separate sound sources, estimate pitch and learn representations from recordings without requiring people to label every sound.

Research topics

  • Machine listening
  • Speech and music processing
  • Bioacoustics
