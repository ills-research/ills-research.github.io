Biography

AJung Moon studies how people and robots coordinate everyday actions. When a robot hands someone an object, for example, where it looks or whether it hesitates can change how the person interprets its intentions. She uses experiments in human–robot interaction to study these cues and examines the ethical choices involved in designing robots that work alongside people.

Research topics

  • Human–robot interaction
  • Nonverbal communication
  • Robot ethics
