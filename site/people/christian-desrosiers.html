Biography

Christian Desrosiers develops machine learning methods for biomedical images. His research asks how a model can learn useful features when only a small fraction of scans have expert annotations, and how well those features transfer between datasets. He studies these questions through tasks such as identifying anatomical structures and detecting abnormalities in medical images.

Research topics

  • Biomedical image analysis
  • Representation learning
  • Learning with limited labels
