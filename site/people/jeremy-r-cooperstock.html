Jeremy R. Cooperstock

Professor

  • McGill University

Biography

Jeremy R. Cooperstock studies how sound, touch and images can convey aspects of a physical environment that a screen alone cannot. In McGill’s Shared Reality Lab, his group builds and evaluates interfaces such as floors that reproduce the sensation of footsteps and tools that give blind users auditory or tactile cues about their surroundings.

Research topics

  • Human–computer interaction
  • Haptics and sound
  • Accessible interfaces
