Stéphane Coulombe

Professor

  • ÉTS

Biography

Stéphane Coulombe studies how to compress and deliver video under limits on bandwidth, processing power and delay. In immersive video, a viewer may turn to look in a new direction before the next segment arrives. His work examines how encoding and streaming systems can use the available resources to preserve the quality the viewer actually sees.

Research topics

  • Video compression
  • Immersive video
  • Adaptive streaming
