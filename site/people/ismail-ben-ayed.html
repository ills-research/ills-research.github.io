Biography

Ismail Ben Ayed uses mathematical optimization to tackle problems in computer vision and medical imaging. His work on image segmentation asks how an algorithm can outline an organ or lesion when few images have been annotated. He studies how to incorporate prior knowledge into the learning objective and how to adapt a trained model to new data.

Research topics

  • Optimization
  • Medical image segmentation
  • Model adaptation
