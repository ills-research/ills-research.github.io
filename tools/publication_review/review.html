A paper appears once when its DBLP publication year falls within the window of at least one listed author. The member filter and CSV credit only those whose windows cover that year. The author list follows the original paper.

Records come from the __AS_OF__ edition of the DBLP bibliography. Years with no indexed papers show zero results.

Preprints use DBLP’s CoRR/arXiv records. A preprint and its published version each count when DBLP indexes both. Journal papers follow the journal venue. International conference proceedings include main tracks, affiliated workshops, companion volumes and posters indexed as proceedings papers. The totals exclude corrections, front matter, seminar reports, software releases and book chapters.
